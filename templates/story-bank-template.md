# Behavioral Story Bank

Stories follow the STAR+R format: Situation, Task, Action, Result, Reflection.
The Reflection column is what separates senior candidates — it shows learning, not just execution.

---

## Template

**Story title:**
**Themes:** (e.g., ownership, ambiguity, cross-functional influence, mentoring)
**Best for companies:** (e.g., Amazon LP: Ownership, Netflix: Stunning Colleagues)

**S — Situation:**
(2-3 sentences: what was the context, why did it matter?)

**T — Task:**
(1-2 sentences: what was YOUR specific responsibility?)

**A — Action:**
(3-5 bullets: concrete things YOU did, active voice, no "we")

**R — Result:**
(Quantified outcome. "Reduced X by Y%", "$Zm revenue protected", "N teams adopted the framework")

**Reflection:**
(What would you do differently? What did you learn? What would you tell a junior engineer?)
